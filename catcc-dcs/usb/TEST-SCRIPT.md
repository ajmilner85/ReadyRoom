# CATCC test: second PC (2026-11-25)

Host = development PC. Client = gaming PC.

## Setup

**1.** Client, DCS closed: double-click **Install CATCC.cmd** on the stick, say Yes, press Enter when done.
**2.** Host: host the mission with a slot on every spot. Start the deck spotting demo with **Spot All**.
**3.** Client: join the host as a second player.

## 1. The lookalike, seen from the client

**4.** Client: put your jet or camera where you can see a parked Hornet on the deck.
**5.** Host: take the slot on that spot (picker, Spawn), then press **FLY**.
**6.** Client, from the moment the host takes the slot until the host's jet appears, note:
- Is there a Hornet on the spot the whole time, or does the spot go empty? For about how long?
- Gear down or up? Wings folded or spread? Board number shown? Right livery?
- Does it sit on the deck, or too low / too high?

## 2. The host's stall, seen from the client

**7.** The host freezes about 6 s while DCS makes its jet. Client: does the spot stay filled through those seconds, or empty first?

## 3. The client spawning

**8.** Host: watch a parked Hornet's spot.
**9.** Client: take the slot on that spot (picker, or DCS's own slot list). Stay in the briefing a while, then press **FLY**.
**10.** Host, note:
- Is there a Hornet on the spot all through the client's briefing?
- Does the client's jet come up on its own spot?

## 4. The slot picker from a remote player

**11.** Client: does the picker show instead of DCS's slot screen?
**12.** Client: pick a slot whose aircraft is still in the hangar: refused, with the reason?
**13.** Client: take a slot, then go back to spectators: does its static come back? Is the slot still held for you?

## 5. The deck demo, seen from the client

**14.** Client: watch a tow (tractor, bar, driver, aircraft). Smooth, jumping, or not moving at all?

## 6. Optional: the launch gate

**15.** Host: run the CATCC room (**Local room** button), sit in PriFly's Shooter or Air Boss seat.
**16.** Client: taxi to a catapult, get ready, salute.
**17.** Does the shooter hold ("Shooter holding: awaiting PriFly clearance") until **LAUNCH** is pressed?

## Afterwards

**18.** Client, DCS closed: double-click **Uninstall CATCC.cmd** (it copies the client's logs into the stick's **CATCC Logs** folder first).
**19.** Bring the stick back to the host and say what you saw.
